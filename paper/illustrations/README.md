# Illustrations for the writeup (not data figures)

These three images are presentation illustrations used in the Kaggle writeup. They were rendered by an image model (ChatGPT) from the author's written specification; every label and number in them was copied from `paper/paper.md` / `analysis/summary.json` and checked against the specification after rendering. They are not outputs of the analysis code. The data figures (`paper/fig1_resolution.png`, `fig2_ladder.png`, `fig3_delta.png`) are generated by `scripts/figures.py` from the released records.

- `design_overview.png` — the pre-registered 3 × 2 × 5 design, the two scaffold levels and the outcome ladder L0–L5.
- `result_summary.png` — the three confirmatory contrasts (E4B +5.4 points, 95% CI +2.3 to +8.8; 12B −2.1 and 26B-A4B −1.0, equivalent within ±5 by TOST).
- `cover.png` — cover illustration, no text.
